Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Last updated November 4, 2026
Operated by The Artificial Intelligence Agency of San Francisco (“SFAI”) · info@sfai.agency

The short version: we use the work you submit only to run your check and show you your report. We never use it to train AI, and we don’t let anyone else do so. Your work stays in your account until you delete it, and we delete everything within 30 days after you ask us to close your account. We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use analytics or advertising cookies.

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Artificial Intelligence Agency of San Francisco (“SFAI,” “we,” “us”) collects when you use Croquis at app.sfai.agency and our website at sfai.agency (together, the “Service”), how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service, and terms defined there (such as “User Content” and “check”) have the same meaning here.

2. How we use information

We use your information to:

  • Run the check you purchased and show you your report.
  • Process payments, refunds, and chargeback disputes.
  • Keep a record of your acceptance of our Terms and checkout acknowledgments.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Fix problems and keep the Service working. This uses technical data only, never your submitted works.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We send you emails about your account, order, and report. We will send marketing emails only if you opt in, and every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

3. Your submitted works and AI training

We do not use your submitted works to train AI models, and we do not permit anyone else to. This includes our own models, third-party models, and our service providers.

Your submitted works are used only to run your check, show you your report, and handle any support or refund request about that check.

Any service provider that processes your submitted works, including providers of AI, similarity-matching, or analysis tools, is contractually prohibited from using them to train AI models or for any purpose of its own.

We will not change this commitment for works you have already submitted. Any future use of submitted works for AI training would require your prior, express opt-in consent.

We do not publish your submitted works or share them with the dataset owners, AI companies, or other third parties named in your report. If you choose to turn on a public link to your report, anyone with that link can see the report, which can include short excerpts of your work. You can turn the link off at any time.

This commitment covers what we do. It cannot stop other companies from collecting material that is already publicly accessible elsewhere.

6. Security

We protect your information with measures appropriate to its sensitivity. These include encryption in transit (HTTPS), password hashing, and access limited to staff and providers who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law. Please do not submit confidential or highly sensitive material that a check does not require.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We keep this minimal. Croquis stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay logged in; the Service cannot work without it. We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies. Our website loads its typeface from Adobe Fonts, which receives basic technical data as described in Section 4.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out where the law requires. Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, there is nothing further to switch off.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your information, including your submitted works and your account.
  • Port your information to another service in a usable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, such as for marketing emails.
  • Opt out of sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling. We do none of these, but you may still submit a request.

How to make a request. Email info@sfai.agency from the address on your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We respond within the time the law requires, usually 30 to 45 days. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. You can also delete individual pieces of work and reports yourself, at any time, in your account.

EU and UK residents. We process your information on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (running your check and delivering your report); our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and keeping the Service working); your consent (marketing); and legal obligations (tax and accounting records). You may complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents. In the past 12 months we collected the categories in Section 1, for the purposes in Section 2, and disclosed them to the recipients in Section 4. We have not sold or shared personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

9. International data transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. When we transfer personal information from the EU or UK to a country without an adequacy decision, we use safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), or rely on a provider’s certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

10. Children’s privacy

The Service is for adults 18 and older, as our Terms require. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact info@sfai.agency and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version with a revised “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify recent customers by email before the changes take effect. Changes will never weaken the commitments in Sections 3 and 5 for works you have already submitted.
