Example reports

Real Croquis reports for 137 artists and archive collections, showing where their work appears in the datasets used to train today's image models.

Every shard of every image dataset searched — 1,964 parquet files, 0 errors. Counts are exact, not estimated.
Credible mentions counts distinctive character and strip names plus the artist's own credit lines. Franchise properties held in an archive, common-name collisions and documented look-alikes are counted separately and excluded from this figure.
MIA is the membership-inference score from probing Stable Diffusion directly, where that was run — 0.5 is the null point. A dash means no model probe was run for that subject.
