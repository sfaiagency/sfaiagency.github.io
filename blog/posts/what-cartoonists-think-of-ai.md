SFAI recently made a trip to Columbus, Ohio for the [National Cartoonists Society](https://nationalcartoonists.com) annual convention and awards ceremony. As [a cartoonist myself](https://sfai.agency/about/), I’m particularly attuned to what it feels like to have your work used to train AI models without consent or compensation.

With that in mind, we thought we’d ask the assembled conference hall full of cartoonists what they thought of AI. What better way to do that than to give them a long scroll of paper and Sharpie pens? (Sometimes low-tech is the best tech.)

Here’s just a sampling of what they came up with…
