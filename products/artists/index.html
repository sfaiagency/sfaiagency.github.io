Solutions

Individual creators

Find out if AI was trained on your work. You get a report with the evidence. Then you decide what happens next. See a sample report.

There are two parts. Protect shows where your work went and helps guard it. License lets you decide if AI can use your work, on what terms, and for what price. Or you can say no.

What you get

Protect

See where your work went, and keep it from going further without you.

  • We look for your name and your work in the public datasets used to train AI. We also test the AI models themselves. Our FAQ explains how.
  • You get a report with the evidence: what we checked, what we found, and how sure we are.
  • We check again as new AI models come out.
  • We can add protections to the work on your own website to make scraping harder.

License

Decide if AI can use your work. It is your choice.

  • Choose if your work can be used for AI at all. Saying no to everything is always an option.
  • If you say yes, you set the terms: which works, which companies, and what price.
  • Get paid. We are building a way for creators who license their work for AI to be paid for it.
  • You can change your mind. Nothing happens without your say.

How it works

1

Tell us about your work

Create an account and tell us about your work and where it lives online.

2

We run the checks

We search training datasets and test AI models for signs that your work, or your style, was used. We keep checking as new models come out.

3

You decide

Read your report. Then choose: deal with past use, protect your work from here on, or set licensing terms.
