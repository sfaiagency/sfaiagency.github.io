Protect

Find out if AI was trained on your work. Our tool, Croquis, searches the large public datasets used to train AI. It also tests the AI models themselves. If your work was used, we show you where, with evidence.

It works for one artist, for a catalog of thousands of works, and for legal teams building a case.

What you get

We find the work

We search public training datasets for your name and your work. We also test AI models to see if they learned from it. Our FAQ explains how, in plain words.

A clear report

Each report lists the datasets and models we checked, what we found, and how sure we are.

We keep checking

New AI models come out all the time, so we check again. For work on your own website, we can also add protections that make scraping harder.
