Product

License

The people who own creative work should decide if AI can use it. With License, rights holders choose which works are available, set the terms, and get paid. AI companies get work they have permission to use.

Nothing is licensed unless the owner says yes. Saying no to everything is always an option.

What you get

The owner’s terms

Choose which works, which uses (training, fine-tuning, or generation), which partners, and what price.

A record of consent

Consent and sources are written down from the start. This builds on the data provenance framework in our research.

Both sides

We connect rights holders who want to license with AI companies that need clean data. The money goes to the people who made the work.

How it works

1

Rights holders set the terms

Pick what AI can use, work by work, and on what conditions. Or keep it all off the table.

2

We match and record

We connect consented catalogs with AI companies that need them. Consent and sources are recorded.

3

License and get paid

Terms are agreed directly. Credit and payment go back to the rights holders.
