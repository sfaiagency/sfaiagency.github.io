Solutions

AI Companies

Some AI companies want to do this right. They want to train on work they have permission to use, and they want to be able to show it. We help with both.

We license creative work from artists and rights holders who chose to make it available. We record consent and sources from the start. And we audit your training-data disclosures, so what you publish matches what you used.

What you get

Licensed, consented data

Creative work in many formats and fields, from artists and rights holders who chose to license it for AI training and generation. Payment goes to the people who made it.

A record of where it came from

Consent and sources are written down from the start, using the data provenance framework from our research. That makes your training-data summary easier to write and to check.

An independent audit

We check your training-data summary and opt-out policy against what is actually in your data and models. You can fix gaps before you publish. How audits work

Why it matters

More rules require it

More and more places require AI companies to publish a summary of their training data and to respect creators who opt out.

Opt-outs need a license

Many creators and publishers have said no to text and data mining. Using their work then needs their permission.

Claims can be checked

A published summary can be tested against the model. It is better to get it right before you publish.
